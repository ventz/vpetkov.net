This is my new website!

As I got older, I had less and less free time. What used to be entire
weekends of spending geeking around now became an hour here or an hour
there each month. Maintaining a heavy, and arguably complicated, stack became a dreaded job.
Even though almost everything was automated, I realized that I was
effectively maintaining an operating system and 5-6+
services/frameworks/technologies just to post how-tos and interesting
information. Ironically enough, as I had less time, more of it was
spent on maintenance, and less of it on actually posting blog posts.
This is evident that in December of 2023, my last blog post was from
Mar 2020.
<!--more-->


Over the last few years, I realized that there is almost a beautiful
simplicity in Markdown and having a static website. You can do pretty
incredible things in terms of deployments, flexibility, scalability,
performance, with practically no management overhead.

At the end of the day, I just wanted something that would allow me to
post quick (and hopefully interesting) posts about whatever I was
working on. In reality, a step above GitHub gists.

So - with that aid, it was time to retire the heavy
Ubuntu+Apache+PHP+MySQL+Wordpress+Theme+Plugins+Updated+Backup+Security+Time/Automation deployment.


Welcome to my new cleaner and simpler space -- a static website,
generated with Hugo. I won't get into the why Hugo, but I did evaluate
just about every option on the market, and I felt that Hugo was the
best choice (for me) out of Jekyll, Gatsby, Eleventy, Hexo, Docusaurus, MkDocs, etc.

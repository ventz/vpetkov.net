My opinion of Tasker in one sentence: &#8220;**_This is the first app that you should install on every android phone_**&#8220;.

A few months ago I needed an &#8220;automation&#8221; app. I was going to default to Locale which I had used when it was still free (this was before there was an official app store for Android), but the $10 cost made me look at my options &#8212; I figured if I was going to pay that amount, I might as well get the best app to do this. My problem with Locale is that it&#8217;s simply not that powerful, it doesn&#8217;t have tons of features, and all the good pluggins, instead of being built in, are additional paid add-ons. I am really glad I looked else where because I stumbled upon Tasker (<a href="http://tasker.dinglisch.net/" data-versionurl="http://blog.vpetkov.net/amber/cache/2626d2542162e31cf3d6374d31b11c6f/" data-versiondate="2020-04-05T07:48:38+00:00" data-amber-behavior="" target="_blank">http://tasker.dinglisch.net/</a>). The official description for Tasker states that &#8220;_Tasker_ is an application for Android which performs [_Tasks_][1] (sets of [_Actions_][2]) based on [_Contexts_][3] (application, time, date, location, event, [gesture][4]) in user-defined [_Profiles_][5], or in [clickable][6] or [timer][7] home screen widgets.&#8221; I am not sure that I can describe it any better, so let&#8217;s leave it at that. If you want to learn more about Tasker, start here: <a href="http://tasker.dinglisch.net/tour.html" target="_blank">http://tasker.dinglisch.net/tour.html</a>

One thing that immediately popped about Tasker was the insane amount of built-in contexts, settings, options, detections, notifications, and actions. One thing led to another, and I started using Tasker for everything. I realized it had so much potential that I went back to stock 2.3.4 and just used Tasker to gain all the cyanogenmod functionality (that I needed at least).

The reason I decided to even write this post is in order to share two &#8220;programs&#8221; that I wrote, which I think are extremely helpful. The first one is called &#8220;Keyguard&#8221;. The second one is &#8220;Blackberry Sound Profiles&#8221;. I am putting these two in their own posts so that they can easily be indexed and searched url wise. I know personally that the most sought after thing on Android is the blackberry sound profile functionality. Well, it&#8217;s finally here! See my next two posts for all the information.
